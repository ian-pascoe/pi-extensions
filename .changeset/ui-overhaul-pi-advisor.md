Advisor now follows Pi's built-in rendering: `advisor_ask` shows a lowercase `advisor_ask` header, a ten-line Markdown answer with Pi's expand hint, and an `Elapsed`/`Took` footer. Interventions and status entries use Pi's custom-message box under an `[advisor]` label with a coloured severity word (`[advisor] concern`) or the state (`[advisor] ● on`) and expand or collapse when clicked, the footer reads `● advisor on`, warnings are prefixed `Advisor:`, and the `/advisor` menu uses the shared settings menu. Requires Pi 1.1.0 or newer.
