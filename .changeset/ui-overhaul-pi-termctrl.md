The `terminal_*` tools now render like Pi's `bash` (header with name and target, last 5 screen lines with the expand hint, `Took`/`Elapsed` footer), Exit notifications use Pi's custom-message look under a `[termctrl] exit` label with a Status Mark per exit and expand or collapse when clicked, and `/ps` and its footer status use Pi's selector frame and shared status layout. Requires Pi 1.1.0 or newer.
