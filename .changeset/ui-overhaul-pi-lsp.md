LSP tool rows, Post-edit Diagnostics entries, and `/lsp` notifications now follow Pi's built-in rendering: `lsp_<operation>` headers, grep-length previews for location lists, the Expand Hint, an `Elapsed`/`Took` footer, and `LSP:` warning notices. Post-edit Diagnostics entries open with a `[lsp] edit diagnostics` label and their counts, and expand or collapse when clicked. Requires Pi `>=1.1.0`.
